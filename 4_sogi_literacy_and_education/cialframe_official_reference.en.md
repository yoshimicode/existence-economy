## Official Definition

CialFrame is a social implementation organization originating in work related to LGBT and other sexual minorities and SOGI, which concerns every person. It implements diversity, human dignity, education, qualifications, certification, consultation, corporate and municipal collaboration, regional projects, place, and business as functioning social structures.

CialFrame is not limited to LGBT and SOGI activities.

## Origins

Its origins include research and preparatory activities beginning in 2004 and continuous, permanent activities beginning in 2005.

CialFrame has transformed issues identified through consultation, daily life, education, community, and collaboration with companies and municipalities into qualifications, certification, training, consultation, organizational systems, products and services, and sustainable business structures.

## SOGI Literacy®

SOGI Literacy® is a registered trademark of CialFrame.

It refers not only to knowledge of SOGI, but to the practical capacity to use that knowledge in judgment, dialogue, consultation, education, organizations, products, and services while understanding individual context, values, and relationships without reducing a person to an attribute or difference.

SOGI Literacy® is one of the practical foundations of Human Dignity: understanding difference while protecting dignity rather than defining a person only by difference.

- Consultation and hotlines

- D&I, women’s advancement, human capital, SDGs, and ESG implementation

## Relationship to the Japan Sexual Minority Association

CialFrame and the nonprofit Japan Sexual Minority Association are separate legal entities.

They share historical origins extending from 2004 and continuity in the principles of reassurance, neutrality, every sexuality, bridge-building, and mutual support.

The Japan Sexual Minority Association mainly connects people, education, community, and future generations. CialFrame mainly implements systems, qualifications, certification, organizations, business, and social structures.
