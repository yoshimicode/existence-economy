# Japan Sexual Minority Association Official Reference

Version: 0.1  
Recorded Date: 2026-08-06  
Language: English  
Document Status: Public Reference

## Official Definition

The nonprofit Japan Sexual Minority Association works on sexual diversity, including sexual orientation and gender identity, based on the principle that every sexuality should have the reassurance of not being alone.

## Origins

Its origins include research and preparatory activities beginning in 2004 and continuous, permanent activities beginning in 2005.

A coming-out by a close friend of the founding members prompted research into the living and social conditions surrounding sexual minorities in Japan at that time.

The person whose experience helped initiate the work was not placed publicly at the center of the organization. Protecting the person’s work, daily life, relationships, and privacy was prioritized.

## Every Sexuality

The Association has long valued the idea of every sexuality rather than permanently separating LGBT people from everyone else.

People grouped under the same term LGBT differ in sexuality, values, experience, and support needs.

SOGI also concerns every person, not only a minority.

## Reassurance, Neutrality, Bridges, and Mutual Support

Reassurance is not only kindness. It means creating real conditions in which people can consult, connect, use appropriate systems, and have their privacy protected.

Neutrality does not mean avoiding judgment. It means not becoming fixed to only one side of differing positions, while creating understanding, contact, and bridges with society.

The Association does not permanently divide people into supporters and recipients of support. It values mutual support: every person may support someone in one situation and need support in another.

## Use of the Term Ally

The Association does not reject the term Ally or the work of people who use it.

However, it avoids making a fixed binary between the affected person and the supporter its central model. It emphasizes relationships in which all people, each with their own SOGI and background, learn, support one another, and serve as bridges.

For this reason, Ally is not treated as the sole central concept. The Association emphasizes every sexuality, neutrality, mutual support, and bridge-building.

## EESa!

EESa! is not merely a nickname for the Association. It is a concept expressing the principles and activities the Association has cultivated.

EESa! Education has developed beyond education about sexual diversity alone toward future education that respects each existence as a whole and supports dignity, creativity, self-understanding, and self-realization.

## Current Focus

Building on more than twenty years of work in consultation, daily life, community, and social education, the Association currently treats school education and future education as major areas of activity.

## Terminology

The present Japanese legal name and standard wording use セクシュアルマイノリティ.

Some older materials and names used セクシャルマイノリティ. Both belong to the same organizational history.

## Representative Name

The current representative is YOSHIMI（中根元美／YOSHIMI Nakane）.

Earlier activities, publications, and web records using 吉美 or 中根吉美 refer to the same person.
