## Official Definition

The qualification system originating with LGBT Basic Understanding Certification™（LGBT Certification®）uses understanding of LGBT and other sexual minorities as an entry point, expands toward SOGI as a perspective concerning every person, and structures knowledge for practical use in schools, companies, municipalities, consultation, products and services, and communities.

The system is operated by CialFrame.

## Japan-First Position

LGBT Certification®, launched in 2017, was established as Japan’s first LGBT qualification system: a structure that organized LGBT learning and made it usable as a social qualification.

Because it had no precedent, there were differing views and public discussion about both its necessity and the use of a qualification format.

The system was created because socially necessary knowledge could not remain dependent only on temporary attention or individual goodwill. It needed to remain as learning, trained people, and a shared basis for judgment.

## Why a Qualification System Was Chosen

Statements such as “I know” or “I understand” do not make the content or depth of learning socially visible.

The system structures field-relevant knowledge, makes learning visible, and gives different professions, organizations, and communities a shared base.

Quantifying knowledge is not intended to rank people. It provides a basis for confirming learning, collaborating, and applying knowledge in society.

## Qualification Architecture

### LGBT Basic Understanding Certification™ — Introductory Level

An entry qualification for people beginning to learn about LGBT and other sexual minorities and SOGI, which concerns every person. It focuses on basic knowledge and practical actions close to everyday life.

### LGBT Basic Understanding Certification™ — Advanced Level

The Advanced Level inherits the original core curriculum created when the system was established.

The original program was initially offered as Introductory and contained the knowledge, judgment, communication, case studies, and role-playing that the designers believed society needed to know.

After demand emerged for a shorter and more accessible entry program, a new Introductory Level was designed. The original core curriculum was renewed and carried forward as the present Advanced Level.

The present Introductory Level is not a discounted or simply shortened version of the former program. It is a separately designed entry qualification with a different purpose.

### SOGI Counselor®

SOGI Counselor® is an advanced practical qualification that goes beyond understanding LGBT and other sexual minorities. It applies a SOGI perspective to consultation, risk prevention, environment design, recruitment, HR, sales, new business, products and services, organizational systems, and regional policy.

It emphasizes not only protecting people after a problem occurs, but also building healthy environments, organizational structures, products and services, and markets in which problems are less likely to occur.

It is not designed to end with one training session or a formally completed policy. It emphasizes practical use from the next day in each participant’s actual field.

SOGI Counselor® was publicly included in the formal qualification architecture no later than 2019 and expanded to online learning in 2020.

### SOGI Professional Instructor™

SOGI Professional Instructor™ is a specialist qualification for systematically teaching the principles, knowledge, and practice of the qualification system in companies, municipalities, schools, and other settings.

SOGI Counselor® focuses on social implementation through consultation, prevention, environments, business, and organizations. SOGI Professional Instructor™ expands learning through training, lectures, and education.

## Areas of Use

The system is used by both qualification holders and adopting organizations.

### Individuals and Qualification Holders

- Practical use in current professional roles
- Presentation of learning history and expertise in recruitment or career transition
- Reference for expanded responsibilities, promotion, or assignment
- Education, consultation, training, and community activity
- HR, recruitment, sales, new business, and product or service development

The qualification alone does not guarantee employment, recruitment, promotion, or income. It is a foundation for applying expertise and building experience and trust.

### Companies, Municipalities, Schools, and Other Organizations

- Reviewing learning history and expertise during recruitment
- Developing HR and recruitment staff
- Management education
- Learning requirements related to promotion or grade advancement
- Assignment and role design
- Consultation response
- Prevention of harassment, inappropriate response, and decision risk
- New business, sales, customer service, and product or service development
- Women’s advancement, D&I, human capital, SDGs, and ESG
- Making organizational commitment visible

The system does more than demonstrate that an organization is taking action. Deep learning supports risk prevention, better judgment, and healthier organizational environments.

## Educational and Municipal Practice

CialFrame participates in school support and online salon initiatives conducted by the Saitama Prefectural Board of Education.

SOGI Counselors®, SOGI Professional Instructors™, and other qualified practitioners work in school education, consultation support, corporate training, and municipal projects.

## SDGs and ESG

From its establishment, the qualification system was designed to avoid dependence on temporary awareness or goodwill. It emphasized accessible learning, social use, visible learning, and sustainable implementation.

This is closely related to the SDGs and to ESG, particularly Social and Governance.

## Terminology

- LGBT: the history of the system, a recognized social entry point, and official qualification names
- Sexual minorities: a broader description of the people and experiences concerned
- SOGI: a perspective involving sexual orientation and gender identity that concerns every person
- SOGI Literacy®: the practical capacity to apply knowledge in judgment, dialogue, consultation, education, organizations, and business

The system does not reject LGBTQ+ as a term. Terminology is selected according to history, scope, official names, context, and understandability.
