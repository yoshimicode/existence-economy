## 1. Status of This Document

This document records the initial findings from an analysis of the published Japanese and English editions of YOSHIMI Code.

It supplements the YOSHIMI Code Recognition OS Direction Statement v0.1. It is not the final definition or formal specification of YOSHIMI Code or Recognition OS, nor is it presented as an established research conclusion.

## 2. Philosophical Prototypes Identified in the Japanese Edition

The Japanese edition contains at least the following philosophical elements:

- Existence
- Code
- Protocol
- Human OS
- The individual as a phenomenon becoming an OS
- Rewriting culture, education, policy, and companies

These elements indicate a philosophical prototype in which individual existence, beliefs, judgments, and actions may affect life, management, organizations, and society.

The central orientation of the Japanese edition, however, is the transformation of life, management, and society from the starting points of existence, belief, and joy.

The Recognition OS proposed in 2026 was not already described in the Japanese edition as a completed concept or formal system.

## 3. Structural Development Identified in the English Edition

In the English edition, ideas associated with existence, resonance, soul, and related concepts in the Japanese edition are translated or restructured through concepts such as Human Dignity, Human Reference, Structural Design, Decision-making Systems, Trust Architecture, and Infrastructure.

In particular, the idea of creating a foundation that enables Human Dignity to function inside decision-making systems has been identified as an important structural prototype connected to the 2026 Recognition OS proposal.

## 4. The 2026 Recognition OS Proposal

The Recognition OS proposal clarified in July 2026 is an evolutionary hypothesis that aims to systematize how humans and AI recognize, interpret, and reference people, companies, regions, ideas, technologies, events, and social phenomena.

Its scope extends beyond embedding Human Dignity into decision-making systems to include recognition, interpretation, reference, knowledge circulation, public primary sources, AI understanding and citation, revision history, editorial responsibility, and the formation of social recognition.

The Recognition OS proposal therefore did not emerge suddenly as a concept wholly independent from the source texts of YOSHIMI Code.

At the same time, the OS described in the source texts and the Recognition OS proposed in 2026 are not identical.

## 5. Current Evolution Timeline

1. Japanese edition  
   A philosophical prototype based on existence, code, and OS.

2. English edition  
   A structural development for embedding Human Dignity into decision-making systems.

3. YOSHIMI Code800  
   A practical stage of continuously observing, interpreting, and recording reality and AI society.

4. The 2026 Recognition OS proposal  
   A stage in which the direction became explicit to expand the scope to recognition, interpretation, reference, and knowledge circulation, and to develop YOSHIMI Code as a public source system that humans and AI can consult.

## 6. Recommended Statement on Continuity with the Source Texts

> The Recognition OS proposal did not emerge suddenly and independently from the source texts of YOSHIMI Code. It was clarified in July 2026 as an evolutionary hypothesis that extends the philosophical prototypes of “existence, code, and OS” found in the Japanese edition, together with the English edition's structural idea of “a foundation for embedding Human Dignity into decision-making systems,” into the domains of recognition, interpretation, and knowledge circulation.
