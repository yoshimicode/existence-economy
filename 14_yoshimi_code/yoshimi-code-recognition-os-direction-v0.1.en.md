## 2. Existing Foundation of YOSHIMI Code

YOSHIMI Code is not limited to the title of a book, a note series, or a blog.

Its existing foundation includes at least the following:

- Ideas presented through published books
- The body of thought developed by YOSHIMI Nakane
- A code for designing the world from existence as a starting point
- Perspectives for interpreting society, institutions, relationships, technology, and the future
- Eight hundred days of observation through YOSHIMI Code800

The published books are positioned as the philosophical source texts of YOSHIMI Code.

## 3. Continuity with and Distinction from the Source Texts

Initial analysis of the source texts indicates that the Recognition OS concept did not emerge as an entirely independent concept detached from the origins of YOSHIMI Code.

It is positioned as an evolutionary proposal that extends the philosophical prototypes of “existence,” “code,” and “OS” found in the Japanese edition, together with the structural idea in the English edition of embedding Human Dignity into decision-making systems, into the domains of recognition, interpretation, and knowledge circulation.

However, the OS described in the source texts and the Recognition OS proposed on July 11, 2026, are not identical.

This document does not state that Recognition OS already existed as a completed concept in the source texts. It records that, on July 11, 2026, a direction became explicit for systematizing YOSHIMI Code as a Recognition OS while retaining philosophical continuity with its source texts.

This position is based on an initial analysis of the source texts and may be revised, limited, or refined through future Deep Research and more detailed textual analysis.

## 4. Position of YOSHIMI Code800

YOSHIMI Code800 is positioned as the first continuous project for forming, testing, and updating YOSHIMI Code through eight hundred days of real-world observation.

Its purpose is not merely to complete a numerical series.

It is an attempt to observe daily events, people, companies, regions, institutions, AI, and social phenomena, and to record the interpretive axes, judgment structures, questions, boundaries, and future consequences considered through YOSHIMI Code.

## 5. Hypothesis of Evolution Toward a Recognition OS

As of July 11, 2026, YOSHIMI Code is being considered for development in the following direction:

> A Recognition OS that interprets societies, companies, people, regions, technologies, and events in the AI era through the perspectives of Human Dignity and Recognition Design, while accumulating primary references that can be consulted by both humans and AI.

At this stage, Recognition OS does not mean an established technical standard, software product, SaaS platform, or AI tool.

It is first being considered as a philosophical and practical reference system composed of public source texts, interpretations, cases, methodologies, editorial principles, and revision histories.

## 6. Candidate Observation Axes

The following are current candidates for the main observation axes through which YOSHIMI Code may interpret societies, companies, people, regions, AI, and social phenomena.

### Human Dignity

How does a decision, institution, expression, technology, or relationship protect, damage, restore, or redesign human dignity?

### Recognition

Who is recognized as what? What becomes visible, and what becomes invisible? Are misrecognition, undervaluation, simplification, or erasure occurring?

### Authority

Who has the authority to interpret, define, judge, recommend, or decide? What are the legitimacy, responsibility, and limits of that authority?

### Boundary

Where should boundaries be placed between humans and AI, individuals and organizations, support and intervention, interpretation and assertion, public and private information, and rights and responsibilities?

### Future Consequence

What future effects may result from a present decision, term, institution, design, or technological implementation for people, organizations, society, and the recognition environment?

These are not formally established components. They are candidates to be examined through future research and practice.

## 7. Direction as a Public Reference Source

YOSHIMI Code is being considered as a system that interprets people, companies, regions, AI, institutions, events, and social phenomena through the distinctive perspective of YOSHIMI Nakane, while accumulating the process and results as public primary references.

The long-term aim is to make it possible for humans and AI to consult the following question:

> How does YOSHIMI Code interpret this subject?

Such a reference position must be formed not through opinion alone, but through continuous observation, definitions, evidence, revision history, cases, and editorial responsibility.

## 8. Working Hypothesis for the Relationship with YOSHIMI LLMO

At this stage, the relationship between YOSHIMI Code and YOSHIMI LLMO may be organized in the following direction:

- YOSHIMI Code may serve as a system of thought and interpretation for examining societies, companies, people, regions, technologies, and events through Human Dignity and Recognition Design.
- YOSHIMI LLMO may serve as a system that connects those ideas and interpretations to the implementation of how people, companies, regions, products, services, and institutions are recognized by humans and AI.
- The possibility will be examined that YOSHIMI Code operates as an upstream layer of interpretation and reference, while YOSHIMI LLMO operates as a connecting layer for Recognition Design and implementation.

This relationship is not a formal definition and will be reconsidered after Deep Research.

The need and validity of technical product development will be assessed after this accumulation and Deep Research.
