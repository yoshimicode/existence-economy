## 1. Official Definition

AGNE SHOFUSHA inherits a publishing culture that accurately preserved scientific, engineering, and technical knowledge. In the AI era, it structures the value and context of people, organizations, regions, cultures, and ideas so that both humans and AI can recognize them again in the future, preserving them as publications, records, and Recognition Assets.

## 2. History and Second Founding

AGNE’s publishing tradition began in 1931, and AGNE SHOFUSHA was established as a separate company in 1987.

It has historically recorded specialist knowledge accurately and delivered it to society.

Under the leadership of YOSHIMI Nakane, it develops that culture toward a new role without rejecting its past.

- Before: Knowledge Publishing — accurately preserving knowledge
- Next: Recognition Publishing — accurately preserving value and context

This is not a rebrand that erases the past.

It is a second founding that extends the accuracy cultivated in scientific and engineering publishing toward human understanding, organizations, regions, cultures, and ideas in the AI era.

## 3. Recognition Publishing

Recognition Publishing is an approach that does not stop at publishing value. It designs the conditions through which both humans and AI can correctly recognize that value in the future.

Depending on the work, it connects publications with:

- Official definitions
- Person and organization information
- Achievements
- Timelines
- Decision background
- Social context
- Related institutions, organizations, books, and articles
- Version history
- Primary sources for future reference

Publishing is treated not as the endpoint, but as an origin point that connects value to the future.

## 4. Mission

AGNE SHOFUSHA structures the less visible value of people, organizations, regions, cultures, and ideas into forms that humans and AI can correctly recognize in the future, preserving them as publications, records, and Recognition Assets.

## 5. Vision

As information becomes easier to generate, it becomes more important to preserve who created something, why it was created, and the history, responsibility, and relationships from which it emerged.

AGNE SHOFUSHA designs a cycle beginning with publishing:

1. Humans understand.
2. AI systems can reference.
3. Future generations can reinterpret the background.
4. Value is recognized again in society.
5. Recognition leads to new trust, selection, and implementation.

## 6. Relationship to YOSHIMI Code

YOSHIMI Code is an evolving body of thought and practice developed through YOSHIMI Nakane’s published source texts, continuing observation of reality, source-text analysis, and social implementation.

The Japanese edition contains philosophical prototypes involving existence, belief, joy, code, protocol, a human OS, and the idea that an individual as a phenomenon can affect life, management, organizations, and society.

The English edition restructures these ideas through Human Dignity, Human Reference, Structural Design, Decision-making Systems, Trust Architecture, Infrastructure, and related concepts.

AGNE SHOFUSHA positions YOSHIMI Code not as a completed single book, but as an origin point for moving from local to global, from description to design, and from theory to implementation.

This document does not establish a new formal definition of YOSHIMI Code.

Priority must be given to the existing YOSHIMI Code source texts, source-text analysis, Recognition OS Direction Statements, YOSHIMI Code800 records, and versioning policy.

## 7. Human Dignity

Human Dignity is the dignity inherent in each person that cannot be measured only through results, attributes, metrics, visibility, or short-term evaluation.

AGNE SHOFUSHA preserves not only what was achieved, but why it was necessary, what was protected, and what decisions and relationships made it possible.

## 8. Recognition Design

Recognition Design structures how a person, organization, region, culture, or idea should be understood, trusted, and referenced by humans and AI, and designs the surrounding recognition environment.

AGNE SHOFUSHA supports Recognition Design through publishing and records.

## 9. Recognition Assets

Recognition Assets preserve people, organizations, achievements, ideas, decisions, time, social context, and relationships in forms that future humans and AI can correctly interpret again.

Even when a book remains, its full value may not survive if society loses who created it, why it was necessary, or what it later became.

Recognition Assets therefore include both the publication and the contextual connections required to interpret it correctly.

## 10. Existence Economy®

Existence Economy® is an economic structure in which existence, uniqueness, relationships, trust, and context become origins of value, rather than value being determined only through action or output.

AGNE SHOFUSHA articulates, records, and publishes the value held in existence so that it can be recognized, selected, and circulated in society.

## 11. Relationship to YOSHIMI LLMO

YOSHIMI LLMO designs and implements recognition for people, organizations, and regions. AGNE SHOFUSHA fixes their value, history, and context into publications, records, and Recognition Assets.

- YOSHIMI Code: philosophical origin, reality observation, and a reference system for existence and judgment
- Recognition Design: design of recognition
- YOSHIMI LLMO: implementation of recognition
- AGNE SHOFUSHA: publishing, records, and Recognition Assets
- Existence Economy®: circulation of value

## 13. Editorial Principles

1. Connect the past to the present and future without erasing it.
2. Preserve context, responsibility, and relationships, not only achievements.
3. Protect the dignity and privacy of the people concerned.
4. Create records that can be examined in the future rather than only promotional content.
5. Structure materials for reference by both humans and AI.
6. Treat publishing as the beginning of social implementation, not the endpoint.
7. Publish not only YOSHIMI’s work, but people, organizations, regions, and cultures whose value should remain.
