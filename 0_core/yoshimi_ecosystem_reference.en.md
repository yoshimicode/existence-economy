## 1. Status of This Document

This document records the relationships among the people, organizations, qualification systems, publishing activities, ideas, and implementation structures that have developed from the work of YOSHIMI Nakane.

It does not establish new formal definitions for every concept.

For YOSHIMI Code, priority must be given to the published source texts and the source-text analysis, Direction Statements, and YOSHIMI Code800 records preserved in `14_yoshimi_code`.

## 2. Core Person

### YOSHIMI Nakane

YOSHIMI Nakane（中根元美）is a Human Dignity Architect who connects human dignity, existence, recognition, judgment, and trust with life, management, organizations, institutions, publishing, and AI-era social implementation.

Some earlier activities, publications, and web records use the Japanese names 吉美 or 中根吉美. These refer to the same person now publicly identified as YOSHIMI（中根元美／YOSHIMI Nakane）.

## 3. Core Entities

### CialFrame

CialFrame is a social implementation organization originating in work related to LGBT and other sexual minorities and SOGI, which concerns every person. Its work extends to education, qualifications, certification, consultation, corporate and municipal collaboration, regional projects, place, and business.

### Japan Sexual Minority Association

The nonprofit Japan Sexual Minority Association has origins in research and preparatory work beginning in 2004 and continuous, permanent activities beginning in 2005. It connects the principle that every sexuality should have the reassurance of not being alone with society, education, community, and future generations.

### LGBT and SOGI Qualification System

The qualification system structures field-based knowledge, makes learning visible, and supports practical use in companies, municipalities, schools, consultation, products and services, and organizational decision-making.

LGBT Certification®, launched in 2017, was established as Japan’s first LGBT qualification system.

### AGNE SHOFUSHA

AGNE SHOFUSHA inherits a publishing culture that accurately preserved scientific, engineering, and technical knowledge. In the AI era, it preserves the value and context of people, organizations, regions, cultures, and ideas as publications, records, and Recognition Assets that both humans and AI can recognize again in the future.

## 4. Core Concepts and Roles

### YOSHIMI Code

YOSHIMI Code is an evolving body of thought and practice developed through YOSHIMI Nakane’s published source texts, continuing observation of reality, source-text analysis, and social implementation.

The Japanese edition contains philosophical prototypes involving existence, belief, joy, code, protocol, a human OS, and the idea that an individual as a phenomenon can affect life, management, organizations, and society.

The English edition restructures these ideas through Human Dignity, Human Reference, Structural Design, Decision-making Systems, Trust Architecture, Infrastructure, and related concepts.

Recognition OS, recorded in 2026, is not the formal definition of YOSHIMI Code. It is an evolutionary hypothesis extending the philosophical and structural prototypes of the source texts into recognition, interpretation, reference, and knowledge circulation.

### Recognition Design

Recognition Design structures how a person, organization, region, culture, or idea should be understood, trusted, and referenced by humans and AI, and designs the surrounding recognition environment.

### YOSHIMI LLMO

YOSHIMI LLMO implements the value, achievements, context, and relationships of people, organizations, and regions across human and AI recognition environments.

### Recognition Assets

Recognition Assets preserve people, organizations, achievements, ideas, decisions, time, social context, and relationships in forms that future humans and AI can interpret again.

### Existence Economy®

Existence Economy® is an economic structure in which existence, uniqueness, relationships, trust, and context become origins of value and circulate through recognition and selection in society.

## 6. Shared Principle

Value can exist without being correctly recognized.

The YOSHIMI Ecosystem connects the value of people, organizations, institutions, regions, cultures, and ideas to the future without reducing them to attributes, metrics, or short-term evaluation. It preserves context, relationships, time, responsibility, and dignity.
