# Recognition Publishing

Version: 0.1  
Recorded Date: 2026-08-06  
Language: English  
Document Status: Working Official Definition

## Definition

Recognition Publishing is an approach to publishing that does not stop at publishing value. It designs the conditions through which both humans and AI can correctly recognize that value in the future.

## Purpose

Even when a publication remains, its full value may not survive if society loses:

- Who created it
- Why it was necessary
- The historical and social context in which it emerged
- The decisions and responsibility through which it was established
- What it later became
- Its relationships with people, organizations, institutions, regions, and cultures

Recognition Publishing connects books, records, official definitions, person and organization information, achievements, timelines, sources, and version histories to form Recognition Assets that future humans and AI can interpret with context.

## Relationship to Publishing

Recognition Publishing does not reject conventional publishing.

It inherits the publishing culture of accurately editing and communicating knowledge, ideas, and stories, while extending the role of publishing in the AI era to include context, relationships, entity continuity, origin records, versioning, and social implementation.

## Core Principles

1. Treat publishing as an origin point rather than an endpoint.
2. Preserve origins, context, and relationships together with content.
3. Structure materials for reference by both humans and AI.
4. Connect the past to the present and future without erasing it.
5. Preserve records that can be examined in the future, not only promotional content.
6. Protect the dignity and privacy of the people concerned.
7. Consider recognition, trust, selection, and social implementation after publication.
